$$\begin{align}
 & -\frac{1}{4}+ \frac{1}{15}= \frac{1}{f} \\
\implies & f= - \frac{60}{11}\text{ cm}
\end{align}$$
The overall linear magnification is:
$$\begin{align}
m & = \left( - \frac{12}{3000} \right)\cdot\left( - \frac{15}{-4} \right)= - \frac{3}{200}
\end{align}$$
Then the size of the image is:
$$\begin{align}
2 \times \frac{3}{200}=3\text{ cm}
\end{align}$$

# Question 6

The frequency of the reflected beam is:
$$\begin{align}
\lambda^{'} & = \lambda \sqrt{  \frac{1+ \beta}{1-\beta} } \\
 & \approx \lambda \frac{1+ \beta /2}{1- \beta /2} \\
 & = \lambda \frac{1+ \frac{v}{c}}{1- \frac{v}{c}}
\end{align}$$
Here the source is effectively moving with $2v$, so $\beta= \frac{2v}{c}$. Then:
$$\begin{align}
\nu^{'} & = \nu_{0} \frac{1- \frac{v}{c}}{1+ \frac{v}{c}}
\end{align}$$
The beat frequency is:
$$\begin{align}
\nu_{\text{beat}} & = \nu_{0}-\nu^{'} \\
 & = \frac{2v}{v+c}\nu_{0} \\
 & \approx \frac{2v}{c}\nu_{0} \\
 & = \frac{2v}{\lambda}
\end{align}$$

## (a)
$$\begin{align}
E_{x} & = E_{0} \cos(kz-\omega t) \\
E_{y} & = E_{0}\cos(kz-\omega t+\delta)
\end{align}$$
At $z=0$, we have:
$$\begin{align}
 & E_{x}= E_{0}\cos \omega t \\
 & E_{y}=E_{0}\cos(\omega t-\delta)= \cos \omega t\cos\delta+\sin \omega t\sin\delta
\end{align}$$
Then:
$$\begin{align}
\sin\delta= \frac{E_{y}-E_{0}\cos \omega t\cos\delta}{E_{0}\sin\delta}= \frac{E_{y}-E_{x}\cos\delta} {E_{0}\sin\delta}
\end{align}$$
Then:
$$\begin{align}
 & \sin ^{2}\delta+\cos ^{2}\delta=1 \\
\implies & \left(  \frac{E_{y}-E_{x}\cos\delta}{E_{0}\sin\delta} \right)^{2}+ \left(  \frac{E_{x}}{E_{0}} \right)^{2}=1 \\
\implies & (1+\cos ^{2}\delta)E_{x}^{2}+ E_{y}^{2}-2\cos\delta E_{x}E_{y}= E_{0}^{2}\sin ^{2}\delta \\
\implies & \begin{pmatrix}
E_{x} & E_{y} 
\end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix}
\sqrt{ 1+\cos ^{2}\delta } & -\cos\delta \\
-\cos\delta & 1
\end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix}
E_{x} \\
E_{y}
\end{pmatrix}=E_{0}^{2}\sin ^{2}\delta
\end{align}$$
The eigen equation is:
$$\begin{align}
 & \lambda^{2}- \sqrt{ 1+\cos ^{2}\delta }\lambda+\cos ^{2}\delta=0 \\
\implies & \lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}= \sqrt{ 1+\cos ^{2}\delta }>0,\ \lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}=\cos ^{2}\delta>0
\end{align}$$
Then the quadratic form is positive definite. Then it is an ellipse.
