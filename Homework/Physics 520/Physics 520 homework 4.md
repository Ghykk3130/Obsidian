As we turn on the magnetic field, the energy levels of the conduction and valence bands are quantized into Landau levels. Therefore, only photons with energy quantized that way can excite electrons. 

Observe that the energy difference between the bottom of the quantized conduction band and the top of the quantized valence band is the smallest. This would corresponds to the smallest separation between peaks. Read directly from the graph that $\Delta E_{g}\approx 0.4\ eV$.
