# 1.1
## (a)
We have:
$$\rho_{x x}= \frac{m^{*}}{ne^{2}\tau},\ R_{H}= \frac{1}{ne}$$
Then:
$$\begin{align}
 & \tau= \frac{m^{*}}{R_{H}e \rho_{x x}}\approx 0.196\ s \\
 & n= \frac{1}{R_{H}e}\approx 5.43\times 10^{23}\ m^{-3}
\end{align}$$
## (b)
We have:
$$m^{*} \frac{dv}{dt}=eE- \frac{m^{*}v}{\tau}$$
Assume that $E=E_{0} e^{i\omega t}$. Then it's easy to solve:
$$j= \frac{ne^{2}\tau}{(i\omega \tau+1)m^{*}}E$$
Then:
$$\sigma(\omega)= \frac{ne^{2}\tau}{(i\omega \tau+1)m^{*}}$$
