
考虑一个由质点$\{ \mathbf{r}_{i} \}$构成的力学系统。假设这个力学系统是有界的，即$\mathbf{r}_{i},\ \mathbf{p}_{i}$都不会到$\infty$。定义位力函数：
$$G= \mathbf{r}_{i}\cdot \mathbf{p}_{i}$$
那么：
$$\begin{align}
\frac{dG}{dt} & = \frac{d\mathbf{r}_{i}}{dt}\cdot \mathbf{p}_{i}+ \mathbf{r}_{i}\cdot \frac{d\mathbf{p}_{i}}{dt} \\
 & = \frac{p_{i}^{2}}{m}+\mathbf{r}_{i}\cdot \mathbf{F}_{i} \\
 & = 2T+\mathbf{r}_{i}\cdot \mathbf{F}_{i}
\end{align}$$
对时间求平均：
$$\begin{align}
 & 2 \langle T \rangle+ \langle \mathbf{r}_{i}\cdot \mathbf{F}_{i}\rangle = \frac{1}{T} \int_{0}^{T} \frac{dG}{dt} = \frac{G(T)-G(0)}{T}
\end{align}$$
取$T\rightarrow \infty$得到：
$$2\langle T \rangle+ \langle \mathbf{r}_{i}\cdot \mathbf{F}_{i}\rangle=0$$

>[!Success] Theorem (Virial theorem)
>$$2 \langle T \rangle = - \langle \sum_{i}\mathbf{r}_{i}\cdot \mathbf{F}_{i}\rangle$$
