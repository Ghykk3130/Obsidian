# 1. Klein-Gordon方程

假设$x^{'\mu}=\Lambda^{\mu}{}_{\nu}x^{\nu}$，并且$\phi^{'}(x^{'})=\phi(x)$，那么称$\phi$为标量场。

若$\phi ^{\dagger}=\phi$，那么称$\phi$是实标量场。显然，它同构于乘法不交换的$\mathbb{R}$。其它的标量场称为复标量场。给定任意复标量场$\phi$，总可以计算$\frac{\phi+\phi ^{\dagger}}{2},\ \frac{\phi-\phi ^{\dagger}}{2i}$。容易证到它们都是实标量场。

对于自由复标量场，宣称lagrangian为：
$$\mathcal{L}=(\partial_{\mu}\phi ^{\dagger})\partial^{\mu}\phi ^{}- m^{2}\phi ^{\dagger} \phi ^{}$$
对于自由实标量场，宣传lagrangian为：
$$\mathcal{L}=\frac{1}{2}\partial_{\mu}\phi \partial^{\mu}\phi- \frac{1}{2}m^{2}\phi^{2}$$
下面展示复标量场的推导。实标量场应该更加简单。

我们假设，任何涉及到类似于$\partial /\partial \phi$的计算都是先将式子经典化后进行的。（在不交换顺序的前提下）。因为以算子作为自变量微分是没有意义的。将场经典化，再推导Euler-Lagrange方程，得到Klein-Gordon方程：
$$(\Box^{}+m^{2})\phi(x)=0,\ (\Box^{}+m^{2})\phi ^{\dagger}(x)=0$$
我们宣称有如下等时对易关系：
$$\boxed{[\phi(\mathbf{x},t),\pi(\mathbf{x}^{'},t)]=i\delta^{3}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}^{'}),\ [\phi(\mathbf{x},t),\phi(\mathbf{x}^{'},t)]=0,\ [\pi(\mathbf{x},t),\pi(\mathbf{x}^{'},t)]=0}$$
对于dagger也是一样。假设标量场都是Heisenberg绘景下的。我们也可以通过Heisenberg EOM来得到Klein-Gordon方程。计算共轭动量：
$$\begin{align}
 & \pi=\pi_{\phi}= \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial   \dot{\phi} }=   \dot{\phi}^{\dagger} \\
 & \pi ^{\dagger}= \pi_{\phi^{^{\dagger}}}= \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial  \dot{\phi}^{\dagger}} =   \dot{\phi}
\end{align}$$
于是：
$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{H} & = \pi \dot{\phi}+\pi ^{\dagger}  \dot{\phi}^{\dagger}-\mathcal{L} \\

 & =  \pi ^{\dagger}\pi+ \nabla \phi ^{\dagger}\cdot \nabla \phi+ m^{2}\phi ^{\dagger}\phi
\end{align}$$
那么：
$$\begin{align}
  \partial_{t}\phi(\mathbf{x}^{'},t) & = \frac{1}{i}[\phi(\mathbf{x}^{'},t),H(t)] \\
 & = \frac{1}{i}\left[ \phi(\mathbf{x}^{'},t),\int d^{3}x (\pi ^{\dagger}(\mathbf{x},t)\pi(\mathbf{x},t)+  \nabla \phi ^{\dagger}(\mathbf{x},t)\cdot \nabla \phi(\mathbf{x},t)+ m^{2}\phi ^{\dagger}(\mathbf{x},t)\phi(\mathbf{x},t)) \right] \\
 & = \frac{1}{i}\int d^{3}x \pi ^{\dagger}[\phi,\pi]\\
 & = \frac{1}{i}\int d^{3}x(-i)\delta^{3}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}^{'})\pi ^{\dagger} \\
 & = -\pi ^{\dagger}(\mathbf{x}^{'},t)
\end{align}$$
类似地：
$$\begin{align}
\partial_{t}\pi ^{\dagger}(\mathbf{x}^{'},t) & = \frac{1}{i}[\pi ^{\dagger}(\mathbf{x}^{'},t),H(t)] \\
 & = \frac{1}{i}\int d^{3}x ([\pi ^{\dagger},\nabla \phi ^{\dagger}]\cdot \nabla \phi+m^{2}[\pi ^{\dagger},\phi ^{\dagger}]\phi) \\
 & = \int d^{3}x\nabla\delta^{3}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}^{'})\cdot \nabla \phi+  m^{2}\int d^{3}x \delta^{3}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}^{'})\phi \\
 & = \int d^{3}x \nabla \cdot(\delta^{3}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}^{'})\nabla \phi)- \int d^{3}x \delta^{3}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}^{'})\nabla^{2}\phi+ m^{2}\phi(\mathbf{x}^{'},t) \\
 & = - \nabla^{2}\phi(\mathbf{x}^{'},t)+m^{2}\phi(\mathbf{x}^{'},t)
\end{align}$$
倒数第二行第一项消失是因为$\mathbf{x}^{'}<\infty$，而积分边界时无穷的，总会使得$\delta$在边界为零。联立得到：
$$\begin{align}
-\partial^{2}_{t}\phi=-\nabla^{2}\phi+m^{2}\phi \implies(\Box+m^{2})\phi=0
\end{align}$$
同理，可以推出$(\Box^{}+m^{2})\phi ^{\dagger}=0$。

# 2. 生成湮灭算符

任何算子$\phi(x)$都可以用形如$\sim \int d^{3}ka_{\mathbf{k}}e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot \mathbf{x}-ik^{0}t}$的形式展开。其中要求$k^{0}=k^{0}(\mathbf{k})$符合色散关系。由于这个约束，$a_{\mathbf{k}}$也仅仅是$\mathbf{k}$的函数。

将本征解代入Klein-Gordon方程：
$$-(k^{0})^{2}+|\mathbf{k}|^{2}+m^{2}=0\implies k^{0}=\pm \sqrt{ |\mathbf{k}|^{2}+m^{2} }=\pm \omega_{\mathbf{k}}$$
由于有两支本征解，我们有：
$$\begin{align}
\phi(x) & = \int \frac{d^{3}k}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{k}} }}e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot \mathbf{x}-\omega_{\mathbf{k}}t}a_{\mathbf{k}}+ \int \frac{d^{3}k}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{k}} }} e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot \mathbf{x}+\omega_{\mathbf{k}}t}b_{\mathbf{k}} \\
 & = \int \frac{d^{3}k}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{k}} }}e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot \mathbf{x}-\omega_{\mathbf{k}}t}a_{\mathbf{k}}+ \int \frac{d^{3}k}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\omega_{-\mathbf{k}} }}e^{-i\mathbf{k}\cdot \mathbf{x}+\omega_{-\mathbf{k}}t}b_{-\mathbf{k}} \\
 & = \int \frac{d^{3}k}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{k}} }} (e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot \mathbf{x}-\omega_{\mathbf{k}}t}a_{\mathbf{k}}+ e^{-i\mathbf{k}\cdot \mathbf{x}+\omega_{\mathbf{k}}t}b_{-\mathbf{k}})
\end{align}$$
其中，$\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{k}} }}$是为了normalization。最后一步用了$\omega_{\mathbf{k}}=\omega_{-\mathbf{k}}$。将$b_{-\mathbf{k}}$记为$b^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{k}}$。将$k$记为$p$。其中$p^{0}=\omega_{\mathbf{p}}\geq 0$。则：
$$\boxed{\phi(x)=\int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}} }}(a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}+b^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{ip\cdot x}) } $$
由标量场的hamilton方程得到$\pi(x)=  \dot{\phi}^{^{\dagger}}(x)$。故：
$$\boxed{\pi(x)=\int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} \left( -i \sqrt{ \frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}{2} } \right)(b_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}-a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{ip\cdot x})}$$
如果$\phi$是实标量场，满足$\phi(x)=\phi ^{\dagger}(x)$。那么显然$b^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}=a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}$。我们得到实标量场的正则量子化：
$$\begin{align}
 & \boxed{\phi(x)= \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}} }} (a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}+a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{ip\cdot x})} \\
 & \boxed{\pi(x)=\int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} \left( -i \sqrt{ \frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}{2} }(a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}-a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{ip\cdot x}) \right) }
\end{align}$$
接下来对于实标量场推导生成湮灭算符的对易关系。容易解得：
$$\begin{align}
 & \phi(x)= \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}} }}(a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}+ a_{\mathbf{p}}^{\dagger}e^{ip\cdot x}) \\
\implies &\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}} } \int d^{3}x e^{-i \mathbf{p}\cdot \mathbf{x}}\phi(x)= a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip^{0}t}+a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}} e^{ip^{0}t} \\
 & \pi(x)= \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} \left( -i \sqrt{ \frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}{2} } \right)(a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}-a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{ip\cdot x}) \\
\implies & i \sqrt{ \frac{2}{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}} }\int d^{3}x e^{-i\mathbf{p}\cdot \mathbf{x}} \pi(x)=a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip^{0}t}-a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{ip^{0}t}
\end{align}$$
容易计算：
$$\begin{align}
 & a_{\mathbf{p}}= \frac{1}{2}\int d^{3}x e^{ip\cdot x}\left( \sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}} }\phi(x)+i \sqrt{ \frac{2}{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}} }\pi(x)  \right) \\
 & a_{\mathbf{p}}^{\dagger}= \frac{1}{2}\int d^{3}xe^{-ip\cdot x}\left( \sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}} }\phi(x)-i \sqrt{ \frac{2}{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}} }\pi(x) \right)
\end{align}$$
那么接下来容易计算：
$$\boxed{[a_{\mathbf{p}},a_{\mathbf{p}}^{\dagger}]=(2\pi)^{3}\delta(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{p}^{'}),\ [a_{\mathbf{p}},a_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}]=[a_{\mathbf{p}^{}}^{\dagger},a_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}^{\dagger}]=0}$$
# 3. 算符的对角化

利用生成湮灭算符，我们可以将hamiltonian对角化。我们有：
$$\begin{align}
\int d^{3}x\pi^{2} & = \int d^{3}x\frac{d^{3}pd^{3}p^{'} }{(2\pi)^{6} }\left( - \frac{\sqrt{ \omega_{\mathbf{p}}\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}} }}{2} \right)(a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}-a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{ip\cdot x})(a_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}e^{-ip^{'}\cdot x}-a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}e^{ip^{'}\cdot x}) \\
 & = \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}}(a_{\mathbf{p}}a_{-\mathbf{p}} e^{-2ip^{0}t}-a_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}-a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}+a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{2ip^{0}t})\left( - \frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}{2} \right)
\end{align}$$
$$\begin{align}
\int d^{3}xm^{2}\phi^{2} & = m^{2} \int d^{3}x\frac{d^{3}pd^{3}p^{'}}{(2\pi)^{6}} \frac{1}{2\sqrt{ \omega_{\mathbf{p}}\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}} }}(a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}+a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{ip\cdot x})(a_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}e^{-ip^{'}\cdot x}+a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}e^{ip^{'}\cdot x}) \\
 & = m^{2} \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}}(a_{\mathbf{p}}a_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{-2ip^{0}t}+a_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}+a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}+a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{2ip^{0}t}) \frac{1}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}
\end{align}$$
$$\begin{align}
\int d^{3}x\nabla \phi \cdot \nabla \phi & = \int d^{3}x\frac{d^{3}pd^{3}p^{'}}{(2\pi)^{6}} \frac{1}{2\sqrt{ \omega_{\mathbf{p}}\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}} }}(i\mathbf{p}a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}-i\mathbf{p}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{ip\cdot x})(i\mathbf{p}^{'}a_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}e^{-ip^{'}\cdot x}-i\mathbf{p}^{'}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}e^{ip^{'}\cdot x}) \\
 & = \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} |\mathbf{p}  ^{}|^{2}(a_{\mathbf{p}}a_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{-2ip^{0}t}+a_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}+a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}+ a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{2ip^{0}t} ) \frac{1}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}
\end{align}$$
那么可以得到hamiltonian：
$$\begin{align}
H & = \int d^{3}x \left(  \frac{1}{2}\pi^{2}+ \frac{1}{2}(\nabla \phi)^{2}+ \frac{1}{2}m^{2}\phi^{2} \right)  \\
 & = \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} \frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}{2}(a_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}^{\dagger}+a_{\mathbf{p}}^{\dagger}a_{\mathbf{p}}) \\
 \implies& \boxed{H= \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} \omega_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}+ \delta^{3}(0)\int d^{3}p  \frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}{2}}
\end{align}$$
注意到，$\delta^{3}(0)= \frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}} \int d^{3}x e^{i 0 \cdot \mathbf{x}}= \frac{1}{(2\pi)^{3}} \int d^{3}x= \frac{V}{(2\pi)^{3}}=\infty$，是全空间体积。第二项称为零点能。我们常常忽略零点能。

另外，我们定义了动量$P^{\mu}=T^{0 \mu}= \int d^{3}x \pi \partial^{\mu}\phi,\ \mu=1,2,3$，或者等价地$\mathbf{P}=-\int d^{3}x \pi \nabla \phi$。那么：
$$\begin{align}
\mathbf{P} & = - \int d^{3}x \pi \nabla \phi \\
 & = - \int d^{3}x \frac{d^{3}pd^{3}p^{'}}{(2\pi)^{6}}\left( - \frac{i}{2}\sqrt{ \frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}{\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}} }  \right)(a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-ip\cdot x}-a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{ip\cdot x})( \mathbf{p}^{'}a_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}e^{-ip^{'}\cdot x}- \mathbf{p}^{'}a_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}^{\dagger} e^{ip^{'}\cdot x}) \\
 & = \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} (\mathbf{p}a_{\mathbf{p}}a_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{-2i p^{0}t}+\mathbf{p}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}+ \mathbf{p}a_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}dat+ \mathbf{p}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{2ip^{0}t})
\end{align}$$
注意到：
$$\begin{align}
\int d^{3}p (\mathbf{p}a_{\mathbf{p}}a_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{-2i p^{0}t}+\mathbf{p}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{2ip^{0}t}) & = \int d^{3}p (-\mathbf{p}a_{-\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}e^{-2ip^{0}t}-\mathbf{p}a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}e^{2ip^{0}t}) \\
 & = -\int d^{3}p(\mathbf{p}a_{\mathbf{p}}a_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{-2ip^{0}t}+\mathbf{p}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{2ip_{}^{0}t}) \\
\implies \int d^{3}p (\mathbf{p}a_{\mathbf{p}}a_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{-2ip^{0}t}+\mathbf{p}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{-\mathbf{p}}e^{2ip^{0}t}) & =0
\end{align}$$
于是：
$$\begin{align}
\mathbf{P} & = \frac{1}{2}\int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}} \mathbf{p}(a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}+a_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}) \\
 & = \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}}\mathbf{p}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}+ \frac{1}{2} \int d^{3}p \delta^{3}(0)\mathbf{p} \\
 \implies & \boxed{\mathbf{P}= \int \frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}}\mathbf{p}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}a_{\mathbf{p}}}
\end{align}$$
# 4. 正交关系

在量子场论中，假设$\cap_{\mathbf{p}}\text{Ker}(a_{\mathbf{p}})\neq \phi$。那么设$\ket{0}\in \cap_{\mathbf{p}}\text{Ker}(a_{\mathbf{p}})$。不妨假设$\bra{0}0\rangle=1$。我们发现$\ket{0}$必定具有零点能：
$$\begin{align}
H\ket{0} = \delta^{3}(0)\int d^{3}p \frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}{2}\ket{0}  
\end{align}$$
并且$\ket{0}$没有动量：
$$\mathbf{P}\ket{0} =0$$
定义单粒子态为$\ket{\mathbf{p}}=\sqrt{ 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}} }a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{p}}\ket{0}$。那么：
$$\begin{align}
H\ket{\mathbf{p}}  & = \left( \omega_{\mathbf{p}}+ \delta^{3}(0)\int d^{3}p \frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}{2} \right)\ket{\mathbf{p}} 
\end{align}$$
代表能量比真空多$\omega_{\mathbf{p}}=\sqrt{ |\mathbf{p}| ^{2}+m^{2}}$，对应质量为$m$，动量为$\mathbf{p}$的粒子的能量。并且动量为：
$$\mathbf{P}\ket{\mathbf{p}} = \mathbf{p}\ket{\mathbf{p}} $$
代表动量比真空多$\mathbf{p}$。所以$\ket{\mathbf{p}}$代表系统中存在一个粒子。

容易计算：
$$\begin{align}
 & \bra{\mathbf{p}} \mathbf{q}\rangle  =  2\sqrt{ \omega_{\mathbf{p}}\omega_{\mathbf{q}} } \bra{0} a_{\mathbf{p}}a^{\dagger}_{\mathbf{q}}\ket{0}  \\
 \implies  & \boxed{\bra{\mathbf{p}} \mathbf{q}\rangle  = (2\pi)^{3} 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})}
\end{align}$$

我们下面来证明这个正交关系在任何参考系中不变：

该引理十分符合直觉。我们来看一一映射的情况。$\delta(f=0)= \frac{\delta(x=x_{0})}{|\partial f / \partial x|}$。因为左右两边都在delta函数约束的位置取到$\infty$。并且对相应的自变量积分为$1$：
$$\begin{align}
\int df \delta(f=0) & = \int \left| \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \right| dx \delta(f=0) \\
 & = \int \left| \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}  \right| dx \frac{\delta(x=x_{0})}{|\partial f /\partial x|}=1
\end{align}$$

>[!Success] Proposition 4.1
>Let $p^{'\mu}=\Lambda^{\mu}{}_{\nu}p^{\nu},\ q^{'\mu}=\Lambda^{\mu}{}_{\nu}q^{\nu}$. Then:
>$$(2\pi)^{3}2\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}^{'}-\mathbf{q}^{'})=(2\pi)^{3}2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})$$
## Proof.

实际上，$(2\pi)^{3} 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})$是一个关于$\mathbf{p},\mathbf{q}$的二元函数。将$\mathbf{p}^{'},\mathbf{q}^{'}$看作$\mathbf{p}^{'}(p),\mathbf{q}^{'}(q)$。我们想要证明两个函数相等。

首先证明：
$$\int d^{4} p^{'} \delta(p^{'2}-m^{2})\theta(p^{'0})=\int d^{4}p \delta(p^{2}-m^{2})\theta(p^{0})$$
注意到$\text{det}(\Lambda^{\mu}{}_{\nu})=1$。所以：
$$\begin{align}
d^{4}p^{'} & = \left| \frac{\partial p^{'\mu}}{\partial p^{\nu}} \right| d^{4}p=\text{det}(\Lambda^{\mu}{}_{\nu})d^{4 }p= d^{4}p 
\end{align}$$
将$p^{'2}$看作$p^{2}$的函数。显然$p^{'2}=p^{2}$。所以$\frac{\partial p^{'2}}{\partial p^{2}}=1$。故：
$$\delta(p^{'2}-m^{2})= \frac{\delta(p^{2}-m^{2})}{|\partial p^{'2} / \partial p^{2}|}=\delta(p^{2}-m^{2})$$另外，可以通过直接计算证明Lorentz变换不能改变$\text{sgn}(p^{0})$。故得证。

接下来，将$p^{2}$看作$p^{0}$的函数。Delta函数的约束为：
$$\begin{align}
p^{2}-m^{2}=0\implies p^{0}=\pm \sqrt{ |\mathbf{p}|^{2}+m^{2} }=\pm \omega_{\mathbf{p}}
\end{align}$$
并且：
$$\frac{\partial}{\partial p^{0}}(p^{2}-m^{2})= 2p^{0}$$
所以：
$$\begin{align}
\int d^{4}p \delta(p^{2}-m^{2})\theta(p^{0}) & = \int d^{4}p \left[  \frac{\delta(p^{0}=\omega_{\mathbf{p}})}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}+ \frac{\delta(p^{0}=-\omega_{\mathbf{p}} )}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}} \right]\theta(p^{0}) \\
 & = \int d^{4}p  \frac{\delta(p^{0}=\omega_{\mathbf{p}})}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}} \\
 & = \int  \frac{d^{3}p}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}
 \end{align}$$
 类似地，可以得到：
 $$\int d^{4}p^{'}\delta(p^{'2}-m^{2})\theta(p^{'0})= \int \frac{d^{3}p^{'}}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}}$$所以
 $$\begin{align}
 & \int \frac{d^{3}p^{'}}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}^{}}=\int \frac{d^{3}{p}}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}
\end{align}$$
将积分区域取为体积微元，则有：
$$\frac{d^{3}p^{'}}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}}= \frac{d^{3}p}{2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}$$
所以Lorentz变换的空间部分的jacobian为$\frac{\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}}{\omega_{\mathbf{p}}}$。所以：
$$\begin{align}
 & \delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}^{'}-\mathbf{q}^{'})  = \frac{\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})}{\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}} / \omega_{\mathbf{p}}} \\
\implies &  \omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}^{'}-\mathbf{q}^{'})= \omega_{\mathbf{p}}\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})
\end{align}$$
>[!Right]
>$\blacksquare$

所以，在一个不同的参考系里面，我们有：
$$\begin{align}
\bra{\mathbf{p}^{'}} \mathbf{q}^{'}\rangle & = (2\pi)^{3} 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}^{'}}\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}^{'}-\mathbf{q}^{'})= (2\pi)^{3} 2\omega_{\mathbf{p}}\delta^{3}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{q})=\bra{\mathbf{p}} \mathbf{q}\rangle
\end{align}$$
