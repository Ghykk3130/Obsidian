假设晶体具有时间反演对称性。那么$\epsilon(\mathbf{k})=\epsilon(-\mathbf{k})$。可以证明，平衡状态电流为零：
$$\begin{align}
\mathbf{j} & = \int  \frac{d^{3}k}{(2\pi)^{3}} e \frac{\partial\epsilon}{\hbar \partial \mathbf{k}} n(\epsilon(\mathbf{k}))=0
\end{align}$$
如果加上电场进行微扰，那么：
$$\hbar   \dot{\mathbf{k}}= e\mathbf{E}\implies \mathbf{k}^{'}=\mathbf{k}+ \frac{e\mathbf{E}}{\hbar}\tau$$
相应地，Fermi-Dirac分布也会移动。原先在位置$\mathbf{k}$的$n$个电子移动到位置$\mathbf{k}^{'}$，平移出新的分布$n^{'}$。这其实就是散射。而平均下来，这些散射只能发生在$\tau$时间内。我们有：
$$\begin{align}
 & n^{'}(\mathbf{k}^{'})= n(\mathbf{k}) \\
\implies &  n^{'}\left( \mathbf{k}+ \frac{e\mathbf{E}}{\hbar}dt  \right)= n(\mathbf{k}) \\
\implies & n^{'}(\mathbf{k})= n\left( \mathbf{k}- \frac{e\mathbf{E}}{\hbar}\tau \right)  \\
 & \approx n(\mathbf{k})- \frac{e\mathbf{E}}{\hbar} \cdot\frac{\partial n}{\partial \mathbf{k}}\tau \\
 & = n(\epsilon(\mathbf{k}))- \frac{e\mathbf{E}}{\hbar}\cdot \frac{\partial n}{\partial\epsilon} \frac{\partial\epsilon}{\partial \mathbf{k}}\tau \\
 & = n(\epsilon(\mathbf{k}))+e\mathbf{E} \cdot \frac{\partial\epsilon}{\hbar \partial \mathbf{k}} \delta(\epsilon-\epsilon_{F})\tau
\end{align}$$
于是：
$$\begin{align}
\mathbf{j} & =  \int \frac{d^{3}k}{(2\pi)^{3}} e \frac{\partial\epsilon}{\hbar \partial \mathbf{k}} \left[  n+ e\mathbf{E}\cdot \frac{\partial\epsilon}{\hbar \partial \mathbf{k}}\delta(\epsilon-\epsilon_{F})\tau \right] \\
 & =  \int \frac{d^{3}k}{(2\pi)^{3}} e^{2}\mathbf{E}\cdot \mathbf{v} \mathbf{v} \delta(\epsilon-\epsilon_{F}) \tau\\
 & =  \int d\epsilon \mathcal{D}(\epsilon)e^{2}\mathbf{E}\cdot \mathbf{v}\mathbf{v}\delta(\epsilon-\epsilon_{F}) \tau\\
 & \approx  \tau e^{2} \mathcal{D}(\epsilon_{F}) \mathbf{E}\cdot \mathbf{v}(\mathbf{k}_{F})\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{k}_{F})
\end{align}$$
所以只有费米面上电子能贡献电流。最后写约等于是因为$\epsilon(k)=\epsilon_{F}$的解往往是连续根，不是只有一个$\mathbf{k}_{F}$。
