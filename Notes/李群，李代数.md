回忆起拓扑流形$M$需要满足：
- Hausdorff, second-countable
- Given $p\in M$, $\exists(U,\varphi)$ such that $p\in U$ open, $\varphi$ defines the isomorphism between $U$ and an open subset in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$.
在拓扑流形基础上，对于任意点取两个chart $(U_{1},\varphi_{1}),\ (U_{2},\varphi_{2})$，如果在$U_{1}\cap U_{2}$部分，$\varphi_{2} \circ\varphi_{1}^{-1}$是光滑的，称$M$为微分流形。直觉上来讲就是可以局部地，微分地变换坐标。

那么李群定义为：

>[!Note] Definition 1
>A Lie group is a differentiable manifold $G$ that is also a group such that:
>- $(g_{1},g_{2})\mapsto g_{1}g_{2}$ is differentiable.
>- $g \mapsto g^{-1}$ is differentiable.
