# 1. 场的lagrangian和hamiltonian

定义lagrangian $L=\int d^{3}x \mathcal{L}$。我们认为，lagrangian density是场以及场的导数的函数，即$\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})$。其中，$\phi_{a}$代表第$a$个场。那么作用量为$\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}$的泛函：$S=\int d^{4}x \mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi _{a})$。

假设在初态和末态，全空间的场是确定的，即$\phi(\mathbf{x},t_{1})$，$\phi(\mathbf{x},t_{2})$确定。对于中间的时空点，每个施加变分$\phi_{a}(\mathbf{x},t)\leadsto \phi_{a}(\mathbf{x},t)+\delta \phi_{a}(\mathbf{x},t)$。那么相应的，作用量变成$\int d^{4}x \mathcal{L}(\phi_{a}+\delta \phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}+\partial_{\mu}(\delta \phi_{a}))$。于是作用量原理要求：
$$\begin{align}
\delta S & = \int d^{4}x \mathcal{L}(\phi_{a}+\delta \phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}+\partial_{\mu}(\delta \phi_{a}))-\int d^{4}x\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}) \\
 & = \int d^{4}x \left[  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}\delta \phi_{a}+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\partial_{\mu}(\delta \phi_{a}) \right] \\
 & = \int d^{4}x \left[  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}\delta \phi_{a}+ \partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}) }\delta \phi_{a}  \right)- \partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})} \right)\delta \phi_{a} \right] \\
 & = \int d^{4}x \left[  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}- \partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})} \right) \right]\delta \phi_{a}+ \int d^{4}x \partial_{\mu} \left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\delta \phi_{a} \right) \\
 & = \int d^{4}x \left[  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}- \partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})} \right) \right]\delta \phi_{a}=0
\end{align}$$
倒数第二步是因为第二个积分是散度，直接变成边界$\partial \mathbb{R}^{3} \times \partial[t_{1},t_{2}]$上的积分。由于在$t_{1},t_{2}$点，$\delta \phi_{a}=0$，所以积分为零。于是得到Euler-Lagrange方程：
$$\boxed{ \partial_{\mu} \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}- \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}=0}$$
定义广义速度为$\dot{\phi}_{a}$。那么广义动量为$\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial   \dot{\phi}_{a}}$。

接下来开始Legendre变换。在任意时空点上对于lagrandgian density全微分：
$$\begin{align}
  d\mathcal{L} & = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}d\phi_{a}+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}d(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}) \\
 & = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}d\phi_{a}+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(  \dot{\phi}_{a})}d(  \dot{\phi}_{a})+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\nabla \phi_{a})}\cdot d(\nabla \phi_{a}) \\
 & = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}d\phi_{a} + \pi_{a}d(  \dot{\phi}_{a})+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\nabla \phi_{a})}\cdot d(\nabla \phi_{a}) \\
 & = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}d\phi_{a}+d(\pi_{a}  \dot{\phi}_{a})-\dot{\phi}_{a}d\pi_{a}+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\nabla \phi_{a})}\cdot d(\nabla \phi_{a})
\end{align}$$
接下来：
$$\begin{align}
 & d( \pi_{a}  \dot{\phi}_{a}-\mathcal{L})= - \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}d\phi_{a} +  \dot{\phi}_{a}d\pi_{a}- \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\nabla \phi_{a})} \cdot d(\nabla \phi_{a})
\end{align}$$
上面等式中独立变量为$\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}$。不妨定义hamiltonian density $\mathcal{H}=\pi_{a}  \dot{\phi}_{a}-\mathcal{L}$。根据统计力学的精神，我们可以将独立变量换为$\phi_{a},\pi_{a},\nabla \phi_{a}$。那么显然：
$$d\mathcal{H}= \frac{\partial\mathcal{H}}{\partial \phi_{a}}d\phi_{a}+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{H}}{\partial \pi_{a}}d\pi_{a}+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{H}}{\partial(\nabla \phi_{a})}\cdot d(\nabla \phi_{a})$$
于是$\mathcal{H}=\mathcal{H}(\phi_{a},  \pi_{a}, \nabla \phi_{a})$。并且：
$$\boxed{\begin{align}
\frac{\partial\mathcal{H}}{\partial \phi_{a}} & = - \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}} \\
 & = - \partial_{\mu} \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}) } \\
 & = - \dot{\pi}_{a} - \nabla \cdot \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\nabla \phi_{a})} \\
\frac{\partial\mathcal{H}}{\partial \pi_{a}} & = \dot{\phi}_{a}
\end{align}}$$
得到hamilton方程。
## Ex:

考虑无源电磁场。定义电磁张量$F=dA\implies F^{\mu \nu}=\partial_{}^{\mu}A^{\nu}-\partial^{\nu}A^{\mu}$。可以根据Euler-Lagrange方程推导Maxwell方程。显然：
$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}&=- \frac{1}{4}F_{\mu \nu}F^{\mu \nu}
 \\
 & =- \frac{1}{4}(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu})(\partial^{\mu}A^{\nu}-\partial^{\nu}A^{\mu}) \\
 & = - \frac{1}{4}(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})\partial^{\mu}A^{\nu}+ \frac{1}{4}(\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu})\partial^{\mu}A^{\nu}+ \frac{1}{4}(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})\partial^{\nu}A^{\mu}- \frac{1}{4}(\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu})\partial^{\nu}A^{\mu} \\
 & = - \frac{1}{2}(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})\partial^{\mu}A^{\nu}+ \frac{1}{2}(\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu})\partial^{\mu}A^{\nu}
\end{align}$$
那么 $\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial A_{\mu}}=0$. 于是接下来：
$$\begin{align}
\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})} 
 & = \partial^{\nu}A^{\mu}-\partial^{\mu}A^{\nu}=-F^{\mu \nu}
\end{align}$$
那么：
$$\begin{align}
\partial_{\mu} \frac{\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})} & =0\implies \partial_{\mu}F^{\mu \nu}=0 \\

\end{align}$$
另外，由于电磁张量的定义，下面的等式自动满足：
$$\begin{align}
\partial_{[\mu}F_{\lambda \rho]} & = \partial_{\mu}F_{\lambda \rho}+\partial_{\lambda}F_{\rho \mu}+\partial_{\rho}F_{\mu \lambda} \\
 & = \partial_{\mu}\partial_{\lambda}A_{\rho}- \partial_{\mu}\partial_{\rho}A_{\lambda}+\partial_{\lambda}\partial_{\rho}A_{\mu}-\partial_{\lambda}\partial_{\mu}A_{\rho}+\partial_{\rho}\partial_{\mu}A_{\lambda}-\partial_{\rho}\partial_{\lambda}A_{\mu} \\
 & = \partial_{\mu}\partial_{\lambda}A_{\rho}-\partial_{\lambda}\partial_{\mu}A_{\rho}+\partial_{\rho}\partial_{\mu}A_{\lambda}-\partial_{\mu}\partial_{\rho}A_{\lambda}+\partial_{\lambda}\partial_{\rho}A_{\mu}-\partial_{\rho}\partial_{\lambda}A_{\mu} \\
 & =0
\end{align}$$
称为Bianchi恒等式。

在$\partial_{\mu}F^{\mu \nu}=0$中，令 $\nu=0$，得到：
$$\begin{align} \\
 & \partial_{\mu}F^{\mu{0}}=\partial_{\mu}(\partial^{\mu}A^{0}-\partial_{0}A^{\mu})=0
 \\
 \implies& \partial_{\mu}\partial^{0}A^{\mu}-\Box \phi=0 \\
\implies & \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\left(  \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t}+\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} \right)-\Box\phi=0 \\
\implies & \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial t^{2}}\phi+\frac{\partial}{\partial t}(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A})-\left(  \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial t^{2}}-\nabla^{2} \right)\phi=0 \\
\implies & \nabla \cdot \frac{\partial \mathbf{A}}{\partial t}+\nabla \cdot \nabla \phi=0 \\
\implies & \nabla \cdot\left( -\nabla \phi- \frac{\partial \mathbf{A}}{\partial t} \right)=0 \\
\implies & \nabla \cdot \mathbf{E}=0 \end{align}$$
接下来取 $\nu=1,2,3$，我们有：
$$\begin{align} \\
 & \partial_{\mu}\partial^{\nu}A^{\mu}-\Box A^{\nu}=0 \\

\implies   &  \partial^{\nu}(\partial_{0}A^{0}+\partial_{1}A^{1}  +\partial_{2}A^{2}+\partial_{3}A^{3})-(\partial_{0}\partial_{0}-\partial_{1}\partial_{1}-\partial_{2}\partial_{2}-\partial_{3}\partial_{3})A^{\nu}=0 \\
\implies & -\partial_{\nu}(\partial_{0}A^{0}+\partial_{1}A^{1}  +\partial_{2}A^{2}+\partial_{3}A^{3})-(\partial_{0}\partial_{0}-\partial_{1}\partial_{1}-\partial_{2}\partial_{2}-\partial_{3}\partial_{3})A^{\nu}=0 \\
\implies & -\partial_{\nu}\left(  \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t}+\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} \right)- \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial t^{2}}A^{\nu}+ \nabla^{2}A^{\nu}=0 \\
\implies & \frac{\partial}{\partial t}(-\nabla \phi)-\nabla(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A})- \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial t^{2}}\mathbf{A}+\nabla^{2}\mathbf{A}=0
\end{align}$$
回忆起：
$$\begin{align}
 \nabla \times \mathbf{B} & = \nabla \times(\nabla \times \mathbf{A}) \\
 & = \nabla(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A})-\nabla^{2}\mathbf{A} \\
 
\end{align}$$
那么：
$$\begin{align}
 & \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\left( -\nabla \phi- \frac{\partial \mathbf{A}}{\partial t} \right)-\nabla \times \mathbf{B}=0 \\
\implies & \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}=\nabla \times \mathbf{B}
\end{align}$$
对于$\partial_{[\lambda}F_{\mu \nu]}=0$取 $(\lambda,\mu,\nu)=(1,2,3)$, 我们有:
$$\begin{align}
 & \partial_{1}F_{23}+\partial_{2}F_{31}+\partial_{3}F_{21}=0 \\
\implies & \partial_{1}(-B_{x})+\partial_{2}(-B_{y})+\partial_{3}(-B_{z})=0 \\
\implies & \nabla \cdot \mathbf{B}=0
\end{align}$$
取$(\lambda,\mu,\nu)=(0,1,2)$，我们有：
$$\begin{align}
 & \partial_{0}F_{12}+\partial_{1}F_{20}+\partial_{2}F_{01}=0 \\
\implies & \partial_{0}(-B_{z}) +\partial_{1}(-E_{y})+\partial_{2}(E_{x})=0 \\
\implies & (\nabla \times \mathbf{E})_{z}=-\frac{\partial}{\partial t}B_{z}
\end{align}$$
取$(\lambda,\mu,\nu)=(0,2,3)$， $(\lambda,\mu,\nu)=(0,1,3)$得到其它两个分量。那么：
$$\nabla \times \mathbf{E}=- \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\mathbf{B}$$

# 2. Noether定理

考虑一个系统已经满足Lagrange方程。我们研究系统在连续变换下的不变性（对称性）。本着李群的精神，我们先研究无穷小变换。

我们先对于场施加一个无穷小变换$\phi_{a}(x^{\mu})\mapsto \phi^{'}_{a}(x^{\mu})=\phi_{a}(x^{\mu})+\bar{\delta} \phi_{a}(x^{\mu})$。之后我们再对于坐标施加一个无穷小变换$x^{\mu}\mapsto x^{'\mu}=x^{\mu}+\delta x^{\mu}$。那么：
$$\begin{align}
\phi^{'}_{a}(x^{'\mu}) & = \phi^{'}_{a}(x^{\mu})+ \left.\frac{\partial \phi^{'}_{a}}{\partial x^{\mu}}\right|_{x^{\mu}}\delta x^{\mu} \\
 & = \phi_{a}(x^{\mu})+\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}(x^{\mu})+ \frac{\partial}{\partial x^{\mu}}(\phi_{a}+\bar{\delta}\phi_{a})\delta x^{\mu} \\
 & \approx \phi_{a}(x^{\mu})+\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}(x^{\mu})+ \frac{\partial \phi_{a}}{\partial x^{\mu}}\delta x^{\mu} \\
\implies \delta \phi_{a}(x^{\mu}) & = \phi^{'}_{a}(x^{'\mu})-\phi_{a}(x^{\mu})= \bar{\delta}\phi_{a}(x^{\mu})+ \frac{\partial \phi_{a}}{\partial x^{\mu}}\delta x^{\mu}
\end{align}$$
其中，我们忽略了二阶小量$\frac{\partial\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}}{\partial x^{\mu}}\delta x^{\mu}\approx{0}$。可以看出，场的总变化$\delta \phi_{a}$一部分由坐标变化$\delta x^{\mu}$引起，一部分由固定坐标下的场变化$\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}$引起。

若系统在这些变换下是对称的，那么对于任意时空区域$\Omega$，要求：
$$\int_{\Omega^{'}}d^{4}x^{'}\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a}^{'},\partial_{\mu}\phi^{'}_{a})=\int_{\Omega}d^{4}x \mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})$$
或写成$\delta S=0$。

为了计算作用量变化，我们先看体积元如何变换。我们有：
$$\begin{align}
d^{4}x^{'} & = \left|  \frac{\partial x^{'\mu}}{\partial x^{\nu}}  \right|d^{4}x^{} \\
 & = \left| \frac{\partial(x^{\mu}+\delta x^{\mu})}{\partial x^{\nu}}   \right| d^{4}x \\
 & = \left| \delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu}+ \frac{\partial(\delta x^{\mu})}{\partial x^{\nu}}  \right|d^{4}x
\end{align}$$
为了进一步计算，我们先证明引理：

>[!Success] Lemma 2.1
>For any invertible matrix $X(t)$, we have:
>$$\frac{d}{dt}\text{det}X(t)=\text{det}X(t)\text{tr}\left(  \frac{dX}{dt}X^{-1} \right)$$
## Proof.

对于任意可逆阵$X(t)$，我们有：
$$\begin{align}
\frac{d}{dt}\text{det}(X(t)) & = \frac{d}{dt}(\epsilon^{\mu_{1}\dots \mu_{n}}X_{1\mu_{1}}\dots X_{n\mu_{n}}) \\
 & = \epsilon^{\mu_{1}\dots \mu_{n}}\left( \frac{dX_{1\mu_{1}}}{dt}\dots X_{n\mu_{n}}+\dots+X_{1\mu_{1} }\dots \frac{dX_{n\mu_{n}}}{dt}\right)
\end{align}$$
其中：
$$\begin{align}
\frac{dX_{1\mu_{1}}}{dt} & = \left( \frac{dX}{dt} \right)_{1\mu_{1}} \\
 & = \left( \frac{dX}{dt}X^{-1}X \right)_{1\mu_{1}} \\
 & = \left( \frac{dX}{dt}X^{-1} \right)_{1j}X_{j\mu_{1}}
\end{align}$$
于是：
$$\begin{align}
\epsilon^{\mu_{1}\dots \mu_{n}} \frac{dX_{1\mu_{1}}}{dt}\dots X_{n\mu_{n}} & = \left(  \frac{dX}{dt}X^{-1} \right)_{1j}\epsilon^{\mu_{1}\dots \mu_{n}}X_{j\mu_{1}}\dots X_{n\mu_{n}}
\end{align}$$
注意到$\epsilon^{\mu_{1}\dots \mu_{n}}X_{j\mu_{1}}\dots X_{n\mu_{n}}$是把$X$第一行替换为第$j$行得到的行列式。如果$j\neq 1$，显然得到零。如果$j=1$，显然得到$\text{det}(X)$。所以：
$$\begin{align}
\epsilon^{\mu_{1}\dots \mu_{n}} \frac{dX_{1\mu_{1}}}{dt}\dots X_{n\mu_{n}} & = \left(  \frac{dX}{dt}X^{-1} \right)_{1j}\delta_{j{1}}\text{det}(X) \\
 & = \left(  \frac{dX}{dt}X^{-1} \right)_{11}\text{det}(X)
\end{align}$$
同理可对其它项操作。那么：
$$\begin{align}
\frac{d}{dt}\text{det}(X(t)) & = \left(  \frac{dX}{dt}X^{-1} \right)_{11}\text{det}(X)+\dots+ \left(  \frac{dX}{dt}X^{-1} \right)_{nn}\text{det}(X) \\
 & = \text{det}(X)\text{tr}\left(  \frac{dX}{dt}X^{-1} \right)
\end{align}$$
>[!Right]
>$\blacksquare$

那么接下来可以证明：

>[!Success] Proposition 2.1
>For any square matrix $A$, we have:
>$$\text{det}(e^{A})=e^{\text{tr}(A)}$$
## Proof.

不妨证$\text{det}(e^{tA})=e^{t\text{tr(A)}}$。显然$e^{tA}$可逆，因为$e^{tA}e^{-tA}=1$。那么：
$$\begin{align}
 &  \frac{d}{dt}(\text{det}(e^{tA}))= \text{det}(e^{tA}) \text{trA} \\
\implies & \text{det}(e^{tA})=e^{t\text{tr}(A)}
\end{align}$$
取$t=1$ then done。
>[!Right]
>$\blacksquare$

若$A$是无穷小变换，那么$\text{det}(1+A)=1+\text{tr}(A)$。所以：
$$\begin{align}
d^{4}x^{'} & = \left| \delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu}+ \frac{\partial(\delta x^{\mu})}{\partial x^{\nu}}  \right|d^{4}x \\
 & = \left(1+ \text{tr}\left(  \frac{\partial\delta x^{\mu}}{\partial x^{\nu}} \right) \right)d^{4}x \\
 & = (1+\partial_{\mu}(\delta x^{\mu}))d^{4}x
\end{align}$$
所以计算变分可得：
$$\begin{align}
\delta S & = \int_{\Omega^{'}} d^{4}x^{' }\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a}^{'},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}^{'})- \int_{\Omega} d^{4}x \mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}) \\
 & = \int_{\Omega} d^{4}x[(1+\partial_{\mu}(\delta x^{\mu}))\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a}^{'},\partial_{\mu}\phi^{'}_{a})-\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})] \\
 & = \int d^{4}x\left[  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}\delta \phi_{a}+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\delta(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})+ \partial_{\mu}(\delta x^{\mu})\mathcal{L}(\phi^{'}_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}^{'}) \right] \\
 & = \int d^{4}x\left[ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}(\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}+(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})\delta x^{\mu})+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}(\bar{\delta}(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})+ (\partial_{\nu}\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})\delta x^{\nu})+\partial_{\mu}(\delta x^{\mu})\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}) \right] \\
 & = \int d_{}^{4}x\left[  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}(\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}+(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})\delta x^{\mu})+ \partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\bar{\delta}\phi_{a} \right)- \partial_{\mu} \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}(\partial_{\nu}\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})\delta x^{\nu}+\partial_{\mu}(\delta x^{\mu})\mathcal{L}  \right] \\
 & = \int d^{4}x\left[ \left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}- \partial_{\mu} \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})} \right)\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}+\partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\bar{\delta}\phi_{a} \right)+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}} \frac{\partial \phi_{a}}{\partial x^{\mu}}\delta x^{\mu}+ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}  \frac{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}{\partial x^{\nu}}\delta x^{\nu}+  \frac{\partial(\delta x^{\mu})}{\partial x^{\mu}}\mathcal{L} \right] \\
 & = \int d^{4}x \left[ \left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial \phi_{a}}- \partial_{\mu} \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})} \right)\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}+ \partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\bar{\delta}\phi_{a} \right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{\mu}}(\delta x^{\mu}\mathcal{L}) \right] \\
 & = \int d^{4}x\partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\bar{\delta}\phi_{a} +\delta x^{\mu}\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}) \right)
\end{align}$$
其中，$\partial_{\mu}(\delta x^{\mu})\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a}^{'},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}^{'})=\partial_{\mu}(\delta x^{\mu})\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})$是因为$\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a}^{'},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}^{'})$与$\mathcal{L}(\phi_{a},\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})$的差异是一阶小量，和前面的$\partial_{\mu}(\delta x^{\mu})$相乘就是二阶小量，直接忽略。

由于$\delta S=0$必须对任意积分区域成立，所以：
$$\partial_{\mu}\left(\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}+ \mathcal{L}\delta x^{\mu}\right)=0$$
于是可以定义Noether流$j^{\mu}= \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}+\mathcal{L}\delta x^{\mu}$。存在流守恒：
$$\boxed{\partial_{\mu}j^{\mu}=0}$$
Noether流相当于无穷小变换产生的流。如果系统在这些变换下作用量不变，那么这些变换对应的Noether流就守恒。

我们可以推导流守恒的积分版本：
$$\begin{align}
 & \int_{\Omega} d^{3}x \partial_{\mu}j^{\mu}=0 \\
\implies & \int d^{3}x \partial_{0}j^{0}+ \int d^{3}x \nabla \cdot \mathbf{j}=0 \\
\implies & \frac{d}{dt}\left( \int d^{3}x j^{0} \right)=- \int_{\partial \Omega} d^{2}x \hat{\mathbf{n}}\cdot \mathbf{j}\end{align}$$
定义Noether荷$Q=\int d^{3}x j^{0}$。那么：
$$\frac{d}{dt}Q=-\int_{\partial \Omega} d^{2}x \hat{\mathbf{n}}\cdot \mathbf{j}$$

>[!Note] Definition 2.1
>Given infinitesimal transformations $\bar{\delta}\phi_{a},\ \delta x^{\mu}$, the induced Noether current is:
>$$j^{\mu}= \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}  \bar{\delta}\phi_{a}+\mathcal{L}\delta x^{\mu}$$
>The corresponding Norther charge in the spatial area $\Omega$ is:
>$$Q= \int_{\Omega} d^{3}x j^{0}$$

取积分区域为$\mathbb{R}^{3}$。不妨假设场在$\infty$衰减为零，$\mathcal{L}$中的动能，势能都衰减为零。那么：
$$\boxed{\frac{dQ}{dt}=0}$$
系统的Noether荷不变。这就是Noether定理。

# 3. 时空平移对称群

考虑给空间所有点施加无穷小时空平移变换$x^{'\mu}=x^{\mu}+\epsilon^{\mu}$。其中，$\epsilon^{\mu}$是一个小常数。在变换前后要求$\phi_{a}^{'}(x^{'\mu})=\phi_{a}(x^{\mu})$。那么：
$$\begin{align}
\delta \phi_{a} & = \phi_{a}^{'}(x^{'\mu})-\phi_{a}(x^{\mu}) \\
 & = \bar{\delta}\phi_{a}+ (\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})\epsilon^{\mu}=0 \\
\implies & \bar{\delta}\phi_{a}=-(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})\epsilon^{\mu} 
\end{align}$$
于是Noether流为：
$$\begin{align}
j^{\mu} & = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\bar{\delta}\phi_{a}+\mathcal{L} \epsilon^{\mu} \\
 & = -  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a}) } (\partial_{\nu}\phi_{a})\epsilon^{\nu}+\mathcal{L}\epsilon^{\mu}
\end{align}$$
流守恒得到：
$$\begin{align}
 & \partial_{\mu}\left( - \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}(\partial_{\nu}\phi_{a})\epsilon^{\nu}+\mathcal{L}\epsilon^{\mu} \right)=0 \\
\implies & \partial_{\mu}\left( - \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}(\partial_{\nu}\phi_{a})\epsilon^{\nu}+ \delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu} \mathcal{L}\epsilon^{\nu}\right)=0 \\
\implies & \partial_{\mu}\left( - \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}(\partial_{\nu}\phi_{a})+\delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu}\mathcal{L} \right)=0 \\
\implies & \partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}(\partial_{\nu}\phi_{a})-\delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu}\mathcal{L} \right)=0 \\
\implies & \partial_{\mu}\left(  \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu }\phi_{a})}\partial^{\rho}\phi_{a}-g^{\mu \rho}\mathcal{L} \right)=0
\end{align}$$
这是因为无穷小平移变换参数$\epsilon^{\nu}$是任意的，可以直接消去。其中倒数第二步我们两边乘上$g^{\rho \nu}$后缩并。于是定义能动张量为：
$$\boxed{T^{\mu \nu}= \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\partial^{\nu}\phi_{a}-g^{\mu \nu}\mathcal{L}}$$
满足：
$$\boxed{\partial_{\mu}T^{\mu \nu}=0}$$
## Ex:

容易发现$T^{00}= \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial  \dot{\phi}_{a}}  \dot{\phi}_{a}-\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{H}$。所以如果时间平移对称性被满足，那么$\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\mathcal{H}=0$。相应的，$\frac{d}{dt}H= \frac{d}{dt}\int d^{3}x \mathcal{H}=0$。能量守恒。
