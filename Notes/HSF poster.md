Kagome lattice systems host topologically nontrivial properties due to their unique crystal structure. Thermoelectric and magneto-thermal effects are great tools to probe the topological nature of these materials. In this study, we performed Seebeck and Nernst measurements on a kagome magnet YMn6Sn4Ge2. Anomalous Hall (AHE) and anomalous Nernst effect (ANE), arising from nontrivial momentum-space Berry curvature were observed. Furthermore, we identified an additional component of the Nernst signal appearing as a large bump feature before entering the forced ferromagnetic phase, more distinguishable than in the electric Hall signals and absent in the field dependence of the magnetization. This feature indicates the geometrical Nernst effect (GNE), which is not observed in the parent compound YMn6Sn6. This observation suggests the existence of unusual spin textures in the compound which possibly generates finite spin chirality, leading to the GNE. Our results reveal the nontrivial topological nature in the electronic and magnetic structures in YMn6Sn4Ge2 and their sensitivity to the Ge substitution.
